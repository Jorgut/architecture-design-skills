# 建筑可视化词汇库

按设计意图选择少量精确词汇，不要整表堆入提示词。

## 形体与空间

- 清晰体块：interlocking volumes, stepped massing, monolithic volume
- 流动空间：continuous circulation, flowing spatial sequence, seamless indoor-outdoor transition
- 中庭：daylit atrium, multi-level void, planted courtyard
- 边界：deep threshold, shaded arcade, recessed entrance, porous ground floor
- 结构表达：exposed structural grid, long-span frame, expressed bracing

## 材料与构造

- 混凝土：board-formed concrete, bush-hammered concrete, fair-faced concrete
- 木材：weathered timber cladding, laminated timber frame, vertical cedar battens
- 金属：standing-seam zinc, perforated aluminum screen, oxidized steel panels
- 玻璃：low-iron glazing, fritted glass, recessed curtain wall, operable glass facade
- 砖石：handmade brick, deep mortar joints, rough-cut stone, honed limestone

增加真实感时描述板块尺度、接缝、边缘、风化、粗糙度和反射，不要只添加“8K”或“超真实”。

## 场地与环境

- dense urban frontage, narrow historic street, transit-oriented district
- sloped terrain, rocky coastal edge, woodland clearing, riverside promenade
- native planting, rain garden, shaded courtyard trees, seasonal vegetation
- realistic pedestrian activity, restrained street furniture, service access kept clear

## 光照与天气

- 柔和日光：soft overcast daylight, low-contrast diffuse shadows
- 清晨：cool morning light, long soft shadows, light atmospheric haze
- 黄昏：warm low-angle sunlight, balanced interior glow, blue-hour sky
- 夜景：controlled facade lighting, realistic interior occupancy, limited bloom
- 雨天：wet paving reflections, overcast sky, subtle water streaks

写明主光方向，并避免在同一提示词中混合正午硬光与柔和阴天光。

## 相机与构图

- 人视点：eye level at 1.6 m, pedestrian perspective
- 室内：eye level at 1.2-1.5 m, two-point perspective
- 鸟瞰：oblique aerial view, site context visible
- 正立面：orthographic-like frontal elevation, minimal perspective distortion
- 轴测：axonometric view, parallel projection, cutaway axonometric
- 镜头：24 mm wide angle, 35 mm documentary view, 50 mm compressed perspective

广角镜头可能夸张边缘。需要真实建筑摄影感时优先使用 28-50 mm，并保持垂直线校正。

## 表现方式

- photographic architectural visualization
- competition-board collage
- physical model photography
- precise line drawing with restrained color wash
- material study render
- atmospheric concept painting
- sectional perspective with program visible

## 约束示例

```text
Preserve the original massing, floor count, window positions, camera angle, and site boundaries.
Change only the facade material from white render to weathered brick.
Avoid distorted structure, duplicate columns, floating stairs, illegible signage, and excessive vegetation.
```
